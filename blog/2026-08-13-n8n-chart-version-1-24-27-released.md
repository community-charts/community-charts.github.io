# n8n chart version 1.24.27 released!

We are excited to share the release of the **n8n Helm chart version 1.24.27** on 2026-08-13, featuring the latest **app version 2.34.5**. In this update, you'll find important enhancements to the Helm chart (version 1.24.27) alongside the underlying application (version 2.34.5). Here’s a quick overview of the changes:
